Smart India Hackathon · ISRO problem statement

PrivAgent: on-device privacy for AI browser agents

A Chrome/Firefox extension that lets an AI agent operate a web page without ever seeing your personal data. Passwords, Aadhaar and PAN numbers, cards, emails and faces are blacked out on your device before anything is sent.

  • Rule-based DOM checks plus an on-device YOLO model running in the browser with ONNX Runtime Web (WebGPU/WASM).
  • Precision 1.00 / recall 0.98, and 0.96 / 0.94 on blind test pages never used while writing a rule.
  • An egress guard re-checks every outgoing request; real values are filled back in locally, only at the moment of typing.

Threat Detection System using AI/ML

A real-time multi-camera surveillance system that tracks people and objects and raises ranked alerts for an analyst: weapons, fights, falls, panic, loitering, crowd surges, unattended objects and restricted-zone intrusions.

  • Trained a CCTV weapon detector with hard negatives (precision 0.86, mAP50 0.83) and a CLIP-based fight classifier (AUC 0.86 on unseen videos).
  • Benchmarked on 56 real surveillance clips: 31 of 32 normal clips raise no alert; 10 of 15 falls caught with no false alarms on everyday activity.
  • Runs 4 cameras at ~8 fps on an RTX 3050, with a hash-chained audit log and 154 unit tests.
